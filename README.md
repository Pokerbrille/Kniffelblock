**Your favorite dice game. Anytime. Anywhere.**

No more paper score sheets or pencils! Yatzy To Go brings the classic dice game to your browser. Whether you're playing with friends, spending time with family, or traveling – your digital score sheet is always ready.

## ✨ Features

- 🎮 **Digital Scorekeeping** – Enter and track your scores with ease
- 👥 **Multiple Players** – Play together and compare your results
- 📊 **Statistics** – Keep an eye on your gaming performance
- 🕘 **Game History** – Review previous games
- ⚙️ **Settings** – Manage players and saved data
- 💾 **Automatic Saving** – Your data is stored locally in your browser
- 📱 **Ready to Play** – Open it in your browser and start playing

## 🚀 Getting Started

Roll the dice, enter your scores, and have fun!

No installation, no account, and no complicated setup required.

## 🔒 Privacy

Your player names, scores, and game history are stored locally in your browser using `localStorage`.

- No registration required
- No server needed
- No external fonts or JavaScript libraries in the cleaned version
- You can delete your saved game data in the settings

**Please note:** Your data is stored in the browser on your current device. Clearing your browser data may also delete your saved games.
